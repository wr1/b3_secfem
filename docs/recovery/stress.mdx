## Same problem vs ANBA

After the [map](/docs/solvers/adapters): same card, `β = plane`, `α = fiber`, global Voigt. Gate: `tests/test_recovery_anba.py`.

On this rectangle, `σ_zz` under Fz matches to machine precision (mean `1/A` on both). Under Mx the RMS of `σ_zz` differs by **1.1 %**.

<img src="/figures/recovery/iso_mx_szz_compare.png" alt="secfem vs ANBA sigma_zz under unit Mx" />

ASYM fibre-on-beam (`ANBA (fiber, plane) = (90, 0)` → secfem `(β, α) = (0, 90)`). Stage-1 `σ_zz` still pins.
