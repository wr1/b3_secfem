This is step 2 of [three](/docs/conventions/walkthrough). The card does not know about the section. The map onto the section is step 3.

The orthotropic card is written in `(mat_1, mat_2, mat_3)` *before* any placement.

<img src="/figures/mat_card.png" alt="unplaced ply: fibre along 1, in-ply 2, ply-normal 3 toward viewer" />

| axis | meaning | stiffness |
|------|---------|-----------|
| `mat_1` | fibre | `E1`, `G12`, `G13`, `ν12`, `ν13` |
| `mat_2` | in-ply transverse | `E2`, `G12`, `G23`, `ν12`, `ν23` |
| `mat_3` | ply normal | `E3`, `G13`, `G23`, `ν13`, `ν23` |

`OrthotropicMaterial.C_local()` is this card, Voigt `(11, 22, 33, 23, 13, 12)` in *material* indices.

At `(β, α) = (0, 0)` the card axes sit on the section axes (`1 = x`, `2 = y`, `3 = z`). That is a [transform](/docs/conventions/placement) fact, not a card fact. ANBA uses the same zero — forward the card unchanged. See [ANBA](/docs/solvers/anba).
